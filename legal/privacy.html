Privacy Policy

Randify is designed to be private by default. All generators run locally in your browser. We do not collect or store the content you generate.

What we store

We do not ask you to create an account. We do not store generated passwords, usernames, UUIDs, or any tool output. Your language preference may be saved locally in your browser (localStorage).

Cookies & ads

Randify does not require cookies to function. If you enable advertising (e.g., Google AdSense), third-party partners may use cookies to serve ads according to their policies.
